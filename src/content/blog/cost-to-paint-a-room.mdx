Hiring a painter for one room costs somewhere between $50 for a closet and $2,000 for a large living room, according to the 2026 cost guides we compared. Most single rooms land between $300 and $1,200 for walls only. Doing it yourself cuts that to roughly $100 to $250 once you count paint and basic supplies.

The guides disagree with each other more than their headlines suggest, so this page shows the spread for every room type instead of one average. It also covers per-square-foot labor rates, what pushes a quote up, what a gallon of paint costs this year, and worked examples (our own arithmetic) for one room and a whole apartment.

<Callout type="takeaway">
- **Pro cost by room:** about $50-$150 for a closet, $240-$600 for a home office, $200-$900 for a bedroom, $200-$1,200 for a kitchen, $600-$2,000 for a living room. Ranges overlap a lot between guides.
- **Labor rates:** $1.50-$3.50 per sq ft of wall in most guides, or $2-$6 all-in. Labor is 75-85% of a professional bill in one 2026 guide.
- **DIY:** about $100-$250 for a typical bedroom (our arithmetic), or $400-$450 for paint and supplies for a two-bedroom apartment.
- **Paint:** one gallon covers about 350-400 sq ft per coat, and 2026 list prices run from about $18 to $131 per gallon depending on the line.
- **Extras:** ceilings, trim, doors, repairs and furniture moving are often priced separately.
</Callout>

### Size matters more than room type

Rooms are priced by wall area, so a big bedroom costs more than a small living room. Facade Colorizer prices a 10x12 ft room at $200-$800 and a 12x12 ft room at $400-$900. ThePaintCalculator puts a 12x12 bedroom at $200-$450 and a 16x20 master at $450-$1,000. This Old House says a large primary bedroom can reach $1,500.

## What painters charge per square foot

Painters quote by the room, the hour or the square foot. Rates are easy to compare only if they measure the same thing.

| Source | Rate | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Housecall Pro (Mar 2026) | $2-$6 per sq ft | Interior painting, aggregated from Angi, HomeAdvisor and Thumbtack |
| Facade Colorizer (2026) | $2-$6, average $3.30; labor $1.50-$4 | Total and labor-only, per sq ft of area painted |
| Fixr (Jan 2025) | Walls $1.50-$3.50; whole room $3-$4 | Whole room includes ceiling and trim |
| This Old House (Apr 2026) | Walls $1-$2 per sq ft; $1-$4 of floor space overall | Wall area and floor area quoted separately |
| Unique Home Guide (Jul 2026) | Labor $1.50-$3 per sq ft of wall | Paint is extra, about $70-$110 for a bedroom |

The key question is which area a rate applies to. A rate per square foot of floor space and one per square foot of wall are different numbers; wall area in a typical room is roughly three times the floor area (our arithmetic: a 12x12 room has 144 sq ft of floor and about 384 sq ft of wall before subtracting openings). If a painter says "$3 a square foot", ask which one.

Hourly pricing is also common. Reported rates run from $20-$50 per hour (Facade Colorizer, InchCalculator) to $30-$60 (This Old House) and about $50 (Fixr), with $75 or more for textured or unusual surfaces. Small jobs often hit a minimum charge: Housecall Pro cites $250-$500, which is why a closet or powder room can cost more per square foot than a living room.

## DIY vs. hiring a painter

The gap between the two is almost all labor. Facade Colorizer puts labor at 75-85% of a professional job, and Sensible Digs and Composite Paint say 75-95%. If you provide the labor, what remains is paint and supplies.

### Worked example: one bedroom

This is our own arithmetic, not a quote. Take a 12x14 ft bedroom with 8 ft ceilings, one door and two windows.

- Wall area: (12 + 14) x 2 x 8 = 416 sq ft, minus about 50 sq ft for openings (20 per door, 15 per window) = about 366 sq ft.
- Two coats: 732 sq ft. At 350 sq ft per gallon that is 2.1 gallons, so buy 3 gallons; at 400 it is 1.8.
- DIY: 3 gallons at $35 is about $105, plus $50-$100 of supplies, so roughly **$155-$205**.
- Pro, walls only: guides put a bedroom at $300-$900. Multiplying 366 sq ft by the $1.50-$3 labor rate gives $549-$1,098 for labor alone, at or above the top of the room-price range, so the per-square-foot figures look high for a bedroom of this size (our inference).

That puts the DIY saving at roughly $150 to $700 against a typical bedroom quote. Time is the price: Facade Colorizer estimates 8-12 hours DIY for an average room against 3-5 for a pro.

### Worked example: a two-bedroom apartment

Again our arithmetic, assuming 8 ft ceilings and ignoring cabinets. We took six spaces and subtracted openings the same way.

| Space | Size | Wall area (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Living room | 12x15 ft | 382 sq ft |
| Bedroom 1 | 12x12 ft | 334 sq ft |
| Bedroom 2 | 10x12 ft | 317 sq ft |
| Kitchen | 10x10 ft | 285 sq ft |
| Bathroom | 5x8 ft | 173 sq ft |
| Hallway | 3x12 ft | 180 sq ft |
| **Total** | | **about 1,671 sq ft** |

Two coats is about 3,342 sq ft, or 8.4 to 9.5 gallons at 400 to 350 sq ft per gallon, so about **10 gallons**. At $35 a gallon that is $350, and with $50-$100 of supplies the DIY total is about **$400-$450**. Sensible Digs (updated October 2026) puts DIY materials for a two-bedroom at $200-$500, which brackets that.

For a pro, adding the overall spans in the room table (living room, two bedrooms, kitchen, bathroom, hallway) gives roughly $1,550-$6,800, and Facade Colorizer says three or more rooms can save 10-20% per room, which brings it to about $1,240-$6,120 (our arithmetic). Whole-apartment guides are tighter: Sensible Digs says $1,800-$3,000 for a two-bedroom of about 1,000 sq ft, and Airtasker (April 2025) lists $920-$3,156 for 1,000 sq ft. Plan on the lower half of the range for walls only, and expect a crew to take 1-2 days versus 3-5 for you, per Sensible Digs.

## What makes a room cost more or less

Ceilings, trim and doors are the most common add-ons, and the guides price them differently.

| Add-on | What the guides say |
|---|---|
| Ceiling | $1-$2.50 per sq ft (This Old House); $1-$3 (Housecall Pro); $0.50-$3 (Fixr, Jan 2025) |
| Trim and baseboards | $1-$3 per linear foot (This Old House, Housecall Pro); $1-$4 per sq ft (Fixr) |
| Interior door | $40-$100 (This Old House); $100-$300 (Housecall Pro); $35-$50 (Fixr) |
| Wall repair | $75-$350 per room (Fixr); minor drywall repair $50-$300 (Housecall Pro) |
| Moving furniture | $50-$70 per room (Fixr) |
| Accent wall | $100-$300 (Housecall Pro) |
| Textured walls | $0.50-$1 more per sq ft (This Old House) |

For a 12x12 room, our arithmetic puts the ceiling at 144 sq ft times $1-$2.50, or $144-$360, which is above the $75-$250 ceiling add-on in our [bedroom painting guide](/blog/cost-to-paint-a-bedroom). Doors vary threefold between guides, so ask whether a door price covers both sides and the frame.

Other factors:

- **Prep.** Patching, sanding, caulking and wallpaper removal add hours. Fixr lists sanding and patching at $50-$115 per room.
- **Color change.** Dark to light needs primer or extra coats. One guide that does not name its data sources puts it at $100-$250 more.
- **Ceiling height.** The same guide adds $100-$200 for ceilings 9 feet and up; This Old House says high ceilings cost more without giving a figure.
- **Region.** Airtasker (April 2025) lists whole-apartment ranges of $1,300-$3,100 in New York, $1,100-$2,500 in Los Angeles, $900-$2,200 in Seattle, $800-$2,000 in Miami and $700-$1,700 in Houston.
- **Season.** Facade Colorizer says November through February can cut rates 10-15%.
- **Old houses.** Facade Colorizer notes that pre-1978 homes can trigger EPA lead-safe rules when paint is disturbed. Rules and costs vary; this is not legal advice.

## How to compare quotes

Two quotes for the same room can differ by 50% because they cover different work. Get at least three written quotes and compare these points:

1. **Scope.** Which surfaces: walls, ceiling, trim, doors, closets.
2. **Paint.** Brand, line and number of coats, and whether primer is included.
3. **Prep and repairs.** Patching, sanding, caulking and what counts as a repair.
4. **Furniture and floors.** Who moves furniture and how floors are protected.
5. **Price basis.** Whether the rate is per room, per hour or per square foot of wall or floor.
6. **Minimums and payment.** Any minimum charge, and deposit terms. InchCalculator advises that a deposit be no more than a third of the total when the contractor has good references.
7. **Licensing.** Some states require painters to be licensed, so check your state's rules.

## Ways to pay less

- **Do the prep.** Clearing the room, patching small holes and taping cost you time, and Facade Colorizer estimates $40-$100 saved per room by handling furniture, outlet covers and taping yourself.
- **Bundle rooms and book off-season.** The same guide says three or more rooms can cut 10-20% per room, and November through February 10-15%.
- **Skip what is fine.** Ceilings and trim in good shape do not need repainting.
- **Pick the tier on purpose.** The price gap between tiers is larger than most other choices, and sales are frequent.

## Painting a room FAQ

### How much does it cost to paint a 12x12 room?
Professionals quote $200-$450 (ThePaintCalculator) to $400-$900 (Facade Colorizer) for the walls of a 12x12 room, with one guide that does not name its sources going far higher. DIY, paint runs $55-$100 per ThePaintCalculator, and adding $50-$100 of supplies gives about $105-$200 (our arithmetic).

### Is it cheaper to paint a room myself?
Yes, because labor is 75-85% of a professional bill in Facade Colorizer's breakdown. The trade-off is time: that guide estimates 8-12 hours for you against 3-5 for a pro on an average room, and a first-timer should allow a full weekend once taping and drying are counted.

### How much paint do I need for one room?
A gallon covers about 350-400 sq ft per coat, according to Sherwin-Williams. A 12x12 room with 8 ft ceilings has about 334 sq ft of wall after openings, so two coats take about two gallons. Add a little extra for textured walls and touch-ups.

### Do painters charge by the hour or by the square foot?
Both exist. Hourly rates reported in 2026 guides run from $20 to $60, and per-square-foot rates from $1.50 to $6 depending on whether labor only or total price is meant. Many also set a minimum charge, which Housecall Pro puts at $250-$500, so ask before assuming a small job is cheap.

### Do renters have to repaint before moving out?
It depends on the lease and your state. Many leases require the original color, and skipping the repaint can mean a deduction. Check your lease and photograph walls at move-in; our deposit guide lists the common deductions. This is general information, not legal advice.
