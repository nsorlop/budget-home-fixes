Removable wallpaper is usually priced "per roll" or "per square foot," but the number on the package is never the number you actually pay — pattern matching and waste change the real total.

## Base cost by room size

| Room/area | Wall area (approx.) | Typical cost |
|---|---|---|
| Single accent wall | 80–100 sq ft | $70–$190 |
| Small bedroom (all walls) | 300–350 sq ft | $220–$480 |
| Living room (all walls) | 400–500 sq ft | $300–$650 |
| Powder room / small bathroom | 100–150 sq ft | $90–$240 |

## Why the real cost is higher than "price per square foot"

Most removable wallpaper is sold in rolls covering roughly 28–35 sq ft each, but usable coverage is lower once you account for:

- **Pattern matching waste**: bold or large-repeat patterns can waste 10–20% of each roll matching the pattern across seams; small or random patterns waste closer to 5%.
- **Corners and outlets**: every inside corner, window, and outlet cutout adds small offcuts that can't be reused elsewhere.

A realistic rule of thumb: buy 15% more square footage than your raw wall measurement, especially with a patterned (not plain/textured) design.

## Ways to cut the real cost

- **Choose a small-repeat or textured pattern** — less waste from pattern matching than large florals or geometric prints.
- **Do one accent wall instead of the whole room** — cuts material cost by 60–70% and still changes how the room feels.
- **Buy from brands selling by the linear foot** for oddly-shaped walls, so you're not rounding up to a full roll for a partial wall.

## Bottom line

Budget 15% above the raw square footage of your walls when buying removable wallpaper, and expect a full small bedroom to land around $220–$480 depending on pattern complexity.
