Painting a bedroom is one of the cheapest ways to change how a room feels, but "cheap" is relative — the total cost depends heavily on room size, paint quality, and whether you do it yourself or hire someone. Here's a realistic breakdown for 2026 prices.

## DIY cost breakdown

For an average bedroom (roughly 12 ft x 10 ft with 8 ft ceilings):

| Item | Cost range |
|---|---|
| Paint (2 coats, mid-range brand) | $60–$120 |
| Primer (if needed) | $20–$35 |
| Brushes, rollers, trays | $15–$30 |
| Painter's tape, drop cloths | $10–$20 |
| **Total DIY cost** | **$105–$205** |

Budget paint (under $25/gallon) usually needs a third coat to look even, which erases most of the savings versus buying a $35–$45/gallon paint that covers properly in two coats.

## Hiring a professional painter

Professional painters typically charge $2–$6 per square foot of wall space, or a flat $300–$800 for a standard bedroom depending on your region, ceiling height, and how much prep (patching, sanding) the walls need.

| Approach | Typical total cost | Time |
|---|---|---|
| DIY | $105–$205 | 1–2 weekends |
| Professional | $300–$800 | 1 day |

## Ways to cut the cost further

- **Buy paint-and-primer-in-one** if your walls are already a light color — skipping a separate primer coat saves $20–$35 and a few hours.
- **Ask for the "oops paint" section** at hardware stores — mixed-wrong tints are sold at 50–70% off and can be tinted again for free in some stores.
- **Rent a paint sprayer** only if you're doing multiple rooms; for a single bedroom, rollers are cheaper once you factor in the rental fee.

## Bottom line

For a single bedroom, DIY painting with mid-range paint lands around $100–$200 all-in, while hiring a pro triples or quadruples that cost but saves your weekend.
