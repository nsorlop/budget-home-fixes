A dated kitchen usually comes down to the cabinets. Before assuming you need new ones, it's worth knowing how far apart these two options really are on price.

## Cost breakdown

For an average kitchen (10–15 cabinet doors/drawers):

| | Painting/refinishing | Full replacement |
|---|---|---|
| Materials (DIY paint job) | $150–$350 | — |
| Professional paint/refinish | $1,500–$4,000 | — |
| Stock cabinets (materials + install) | — | $4,000–$10,000 |
| Semi-custom/custom cabinets | — | $10,000–$25,000+ |

Even professionally refinished cabinets typically cost 60–80% less than replacement — the gap is rarely close.

## What DIY painting actually involves

This isn't a quick weekend job if done properly: removing doors and hardware, degreasing, sanding or deglossing, priming with a bonding primer, then 2 coats of cabinet-grade paint (not wall paint, which chips under daily use). Skipping the degrease/sand step is the #1 reason DIY cabinet paint jobs fail within a year.

## When replacement is worth the extra cost

- The cabinet boxes themselves are damaged (water damage, particleboard swelling, broken frames) — paint can't fix structural issues.
- You want a completely different layout, not just a different color.
- You're already gutting the kitchen for other reasons (plumbing, electrical) where the incremental cost of new cabinets is smaller relative to the whole project.

## When painting is the clear winner

- The cabinet boxes are solid wood or good-condition plywood and just look outdated.
- You're renting or plan to sell/move within a few years — painting is reversible-ish (repaintable) and doesn't require permits.
- Budget is the primary constraint — this is consistently the highest-impact-per-dollar kitchen upgrade.

## Bottom line

Painting or professionally refinishing cabinets costs roughly 10–20% of full replacement for a similar visual impact, as long as the boxes themselves are structurally sound. Get a contractor to check the boxes before committing either way — that's the deciding factor, not preference.
